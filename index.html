Welcome!

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Economics at the University of Washington in Seattle. Broadly, I am interested in using causal inference techniques to answer interesting questions. My research lies in the intersection of public and labor economics. My job market paper studies how firms responded to a tax cut for pass-through businesses. Before starting a Ph.D., I completed a Bachelor's degree in Economics with minors in Mathematics and Chinese at Michigan State University.
